Why a sachet?

Each pack is one pre-portioned 32 g serving, so there's no scooping, no mess and no jar to carry. Tear it open, squeeze it out and you're done, whether you're at your desk, in the gym or on a train.

What makes it different?

It's peanut butter with whey protein blended in, so you get the familiar nutty taste plus an extra protein boost in every sachet. Roasted peanuts make up the biggest share of the blend.

Good to know

Contains peanuts and milk-based ingredients (butter and whey protein). Please check the pack for the full ingredients list, allergen information and storage instructions before you eat.
